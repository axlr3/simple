Who We Are

Meet the Founder

👤

Nick Rettenmyer

Nick Rettenmyer

Nick is the founder of Pay Simplicity and brings highly unique personal and professional experience to his clients and partners. These experiences span companies in a variety of industries, in countries in every region of the world.

Nick has lived abroad, worked, and traveled extensively around the world, and deeply understands cultural differences and different approaches to work and life.

His background is strongly influenced by his lifelong involvement in high level sport and athletics. These competitive experiences ingrained the connection, confidence, and resilience that sports develop.

Nick's business career spans two decades of compensation-related experience solving challenging problems. His portfolio includes a strong track record of innovation with some of the world's most recognizable brands.

Nick started Pay Simplicity to take employee pay strategy, design, and communications from routine to compelling and to open the black box of corporate pay, bridge perception gaps, and foster pay advocacy and fairness.

B.S. in Marketing, University of South Florida. M.B.A., University of Texas at Austin — Red McCombs School of Business.

Discover ways to inspire attendees and improve pay fluency & advocacy

San Diego, CA. What makes a Total Rewards program equitable and inclusive? Learn how embedding E&I requires an "activist management" approach across benefits, compensation, and career development.
WorldatWork 2023 — Kindergarten Principles for Rewards▼
Get straightforward guidance to revamp your total rewards program using fundamental principles of clarity, transparency, and purpose.
WorldatWork Webinar — Pay Equity Trends & Practices▼
Leveraging insights from 100+ pay equity analyses, trends and practices from organizations "getting it right." Attendees walk away empowered to address pay equity in a lasting way.
WorldatWork 2020 — Is Skill-Based Pay 2.0 Right?▼
Explore if skill-based pay 2.0 is the right model to support today's turbulent pace of change in the evolving definition of work and jobs.
CHRO Exchange — Reinventing Rewards & Recognition▼
Panel discussion on managing intrinsic and extrinsic motivation through agile rewards strategies and leveraging data for employee-centric programs.
